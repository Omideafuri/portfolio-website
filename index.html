01 POINT OF VIEW

Design Philosophy

THE APPROACH

Minimal. Distinctive.
Tactile. Experimental.
Purposeful.

I have a background in English Literature, which evolved into a deep interest in technology, design, and digital products. My work sits at the intersection of storytelling, visual design, and interactive frontend implementation.

I enjoy taking an idea through the entire lifecycle: Concept → Direction → Design → Interaction → Code → Finished Product.

I care about more than making interfaces look good. I care about how people understand a product, how a brand communicates, and how a digital experience feels in the hands of the user.

"I dislike generic SaaS layouts, meaningless glassmorphism, and excessive templates. I prefer strong typography, intentional whitespace, cinematic imagery, and subtle, tactile interactions."
